# Truth Boundary

## Real in v0.1

- deterministic mandate semantics;
- proposal/refusal logic;
- market freshness/confidence normalization interface;
- evidence summaries;
- explicit mandate review eligibility;
- explicit authorized transition;
- static consumer demo;
- tests proving fail-closed paths.

## Not yet real

- live Pyth retrieval in this repository;
- Solana devnet deployment;
- brokerage or custodian integration;
- real minor securities execution;
- real family identity verification;
- automatic legal handoff at age of majority;
- venue acceptance of a KEYS record;
- any claim that KEYS measures “financial maturity” or “investment competence.”

## Required language

Use **Practice**, **Proposal**, **Mandate**, **Review**, **Evidence**, **Eligible / Ineligible / Unknown**.

Avoid **certified investor**, **competence score**, **safe investment**, **approved security**, or claims that an SPL balance proves legal ownership of a conventional share.

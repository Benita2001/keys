# KEYS — Concept Lock v0.1

## Product thesis

**Financial independence should not happen all at once.**

KEYS is a progressive financial stewardship system. It gives a young person an explicit current mandate, lets them make pre-outcome proposals with reasons, preserves what happened, and supports explicit transitions to wider authority over time.

The durable product problem is not “financial literacy.” It is that families have no strong middle layer between supervision and independence.

## Core primitive

**Evidence-backed progressive authority.**

Evidence can make a mandate eligible for review. Evidence never silently decides that a minor is “financially mature,” and it never auto-grants real financial authority.

## Durable objects

- **Stake** — the relationship around capital: beneficiary, purpose, funding provenance, legal/control truth, representation, history.
- **Charter** — the agreed rules for how authority may evolve.
- **Mandate** — what the beneficiary may currently do.
- **Proposal** — what the beneficiary wants to do and why, committed before the outcome is known.
- **Market evidence** — external evidence used to contextualize and review the proposal.
- **Review** — what was believed, what changed, whether the process stayed inside the mandate.
- **Transition** — an explicit authorized change to the mandate.

## Stages

LEARN → PRACTICE → PROPOSE → BOUNDED → INDEPENDENT

The same Stake can survive these stage changes. The authority changes; the relationship history does not.

## Non-goals

KEYS is not a youth brokerage, a Greenlight clone, a financial-competence score, an NFT badge system, a leaderboard, a trading game, or an automatic maturity classifier.

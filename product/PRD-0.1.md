# KEYS PRD 0.1

## User promise

**Financial independence shouldn't happen all at once.**

A young person should be able to learn, practice, propose and eventually act inside explicit financial boundaries without pretending that practice is custody or that an algorithm can certify maturity.

## Primary wedge

A teen and guardian use KEYS to move from `PROPOSE` toward bounded authority through explicit mandate reviews backed by a durable record of decisions and market context.

## Main user journey

1. Family creates a Stake and Charter.
2. Current Mandate is visible in plain language.
3. Young person makes a Proposal before the outcome is known.
4. KEYS checks the Proposal against the Mandate and current market evidence.
5. KEYS may ALLOW a practice action, ESCALATE for review, or REFUSE.
6. Reviews and market-event reflections append to the record.
7. The record may become eligible for a Mandate review.
8. Real authority never expands silently; an authorized transition is required.
9. Real execution additionally requires current eligibility evidence.

## Product invariants

- Proposal is not authority.
- Evidence is not maturity.
- Profit is not decision quality.
- Silence is not consent.
- UNKNOWN is not eligible.
- Practice is not custody.
- A token balance is not automatically conventional shareholder title.
- Minor private data is not public-chain data.

## v0.1 success criteria

The demo must make a non-technical judge understand in under three minutes that KEYS solves a transition-of-authority problem, not a financial-literacy-content problem.
